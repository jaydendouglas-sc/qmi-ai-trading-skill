# Indicator Guide

QMI uses indicators as evidence, not guarantees.

- **SMA200:** long-term BTC trend filter and hard exposure cap.
- **SMA50 / EMA21 / EMA55:** medium- and short-term trend structure.
- **ADX:** trend-strength confirmation; direction comes from price structure.
- **RSI:** momentum band, with reduced scores in overextended conditions.
- **MACD histogram direction:** momentum acceleration or deceleration.
- **Rate of change:** recent directional strength.
- **Volume / MA20 and OBV:** participation confirmation.
- **ETH/BTC:** relative risk appetite between ETH and BTC.
- **BTC dominance:** broad rotation context.
- **Fear & Greed:** contrarian/crowding context, not an entry trigger by itself.
- **Funding and open interest:** derivative-positioning conditions.
- **ATR and realized-volatility percentile:** execution and risk conditions.

Indicator calculations must use consistent timestamps and venues. Mixing delayed and live feeds can create false confidence.
