# FAQ

## Is this a predictive AI model?
No. It is an explainable rules-based Skill designed to be executed by an AI Agent.

## Does it place trades?
No. It returns a target allocation and action statement.

## Why include cash?
Cash is an explicit risk asset in the allocation. It prevents every regime from being forced fully invested.

## Can other assets be added?
Yes, after defining liquidity requirements, factor mappings, caps and tests.

## Why no backtest screenshot?
A decorative or invented screenshot would weaken reviewability. This version makes no performance claim until a reproducible test exists.

## Is leverage required?
No. The reference implementation is unleveraged.
